**TRADES:**

* Veto Process:  
  * All trades will have the opportunity to be vetoed. For a trade to get vetoed, 7 out of the 10 teams not involved in the trade will need to vote veto.  
* Draft Picks:  
  * You can only trade draft picks up to 2 years away. For example:  
    * During the 2023 season, you can only trade draft picks for 2024 and 2025\.   
    * Once the 2024 season officially begins week 1, you can trade up to 2026 draft picks, etc.  
    * Sleeper does not give me much control over picks, so you will still see picks for 3 years out. If a trade gets accepted with draft picks 3 years out, I will auto-veto the trade.

**INCOMPLETE LINEUPS:**

If you do not set a full lineup of active players, you will be fined $50. That $50 will go to the 1st place prize for the end of that year.

That includes QB. If all your QBs are hurt and you can't pick one up, your options are to trade for one or accept the fine.

\*EXCEPTION\* If it is determined that the team made a good faith effort to acquire a QB through trades but could not align on a fair deal with other managers, the fine will be reduced to $10.

NOTE: If you start an incomplete lineup and have 0 options on your bench for that position, you will NOT take a 0 in Max Points For. Instead, you will take the highest scoring player from ANY team at that position for that particular week.
